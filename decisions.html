Decisions

One argued decision per phase, each with the reason and the alternative I dropped.

Concept

Phones stay outside the gate: the festival is about going offline to reconnect.

Why: The cause is that people feel connected through screens but are not. Going offline is the one thing that no usual festival does, so it is the difference to my named case, music festivals.

Alternative I dropped: The blank wooden phone that is swapped at the gate and used as a ticket.

Direction · mid-pitch

Go with the moodboard direction “Belonging. Togetherness. Grounded.”

Why: The three words give me a test I can use on every later choice: colours, type, mark and tone. They also match the tone of the festival: inviting, playful and hands-on, never a support group for lonely people.

Alternative I dropped: The other two directions: connection with others, and learning a new skill. Both describe a feature of the festival, not a feeling for Emma.

Medium

The core touchpoint is a small campfire object on café tables, with a QR code to a one-page site.

Why: An Instagram ad sits on the phone Emma is already holding. An object on her table gives her a place to put the phone away and only opens the festival when she scans the code. WC5 also asks for a touchpoint in public space before the festival.

Alternative I dropped: An Instagram ad (the alternative I had to name in D-3). I also dropped a first idea of a ritual at the festival gate, because that only reaches people who already have a ticket.

Identity

Separate and enlarge the heart, use Fraunces and DM Sans, and keep the accent green away from text.

Why: The D-2 feedback said the heart was lost. The new fonts match the soft wordmark and the “handmade without being childish” tone. The accent green has only 2.62:1 contrast on cream, so it is decoration only.

Alternative I dropped: The connected heart; Caveat and Special Elite; green headings and links.

Prototype

Show the object as a labelled AI concept image and make the page real.

Why: I did not have time to build a physical model, and I did not want to fake a test. The page and the QR code are real and can be used today, so the key moment can be tried. The fake part is stated openly in the pitch and in every caption.

Alternative I dropped: A cardboard or paper model of the campfire.

Did the decisions work?

The medium decision is the one I check with people. The result is on the feedback page.
