Round 2: dorm test (hallway test)Object: 3 of 3 understood it and scanned the code. Page: 3 of 3 found the answer, nothing unclear. Poster: 1 of 3 understood it, 2 partly (one took it for a sports club, one wondered if it was a music festival or a workshop weekend). All three chose Belonging, Togetherness and Grounded. Would go: yes, definitely, maybe. Quotes: see the D-5 test report.The final assets were not changed after this round. The poster is the open point: the next version would say, in a few words, what you do at the festival.

What I did not follow

  • The poster after the hallway test: two of three hesitated about what kind of event it is. I left the final poster as it is and noted it as the first point for the next version.
  • The AI assistant’s versions of the separated heart. They changed my paper-cut shapes (an extra point on the fire, a longer heart tip), so I did not use them and made the change myself.
  • A six-task test plan with many observation points. It was too long for the time I have, so I kept three tasks and three questions.
  • The injection-moulding tooling costs from the first cost research. I only need a pilot of about 50 pieces, so the plan uses 3D printing.
