Go offline. Reconnect.

How the Get a Hobby! Festival got from a first idea to a final package, including the dead ends.

The cause

Young people aged 21–35 are lonelier than ever. Social media gives them a fake feeling of being connected, so they stop looking for real places to meet.

The festival is one weekend in Brussels where young adults try hobbies they have never done before, in workshops where everyone is a beginner. Phones stay outside the gate.

The story in five steps

ConceptCause, Emma, first idea: a blank wooden phone as ticket. Dropped the same day.
DirectionThree moodboards, one chosen: Belonging, Togetherness, Grounded.
IdentityHand-cut paper campfire with a heart, wordmark, four colours.
Touchpoint and prototypeA campfire object on café tables and a QR landing page v1 → v2.
Final packageCampaign, motion piece, brand guidelines, test with people.
